## Round 3 Hunt List Verification Summary

- **Auth & Session Persistence**: Header state driven reactively by `AuthContext`. Stays logged in on page refresh and across tabs via localStorage JWT token and `/auth/me` validation.
- **Role Isolation & IDOR**: Customer cannot access `/staff`, `/admin`, or foreign reservations/payments/guests (403 Forbidden). Receptionist cannot access admin endpoints (`/reports/revenue`, `/reports/occupancy`, `/users` deletion).
- **Date & Timezone Discipline**: All stay dates normalized to UTC midnight. Formatted consistently with `{ timeZone: 'UTC' }` and localized in IST.
- **Financial & Payment Accuracy**: Server calculates all rates and balances (`computeFinancials`). Client totals never trusted. No real card numbers or CVV collected. Demo payment gateway simulated latency and error handling with clear user guidance.
- **Accessibility & Design Tokens**: 100% compliant with Impeccable design system (`npx impeccable detect` returned 0 errors). Ink on paper palette, 1px rules, Young Serif display, Schibsted Grotesk UI, tabular numbers, and zero AI visual anti-patterns.
